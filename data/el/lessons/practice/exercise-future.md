# Unit 11-12: The Future & Stems

This exercise is divided into four fluency-building activities to move you from theory to automaticity.

## 1. The Constrained Picture Exercise

*(A rainy street in Thessaloniki, people hurrying with umbrellas)*
![Rainy street in Thessaloniki](/assets/images/thessaloniki_rainy_street.png)

**Power Lexis:**

| Greek | Pronunciation | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Θα** | *(tha)* | will (future particle) |
| **Αύριο** | *(áv-ri-o)* | tomorrow |
| **Ο καιρός** | *(o kai-rós)* | the weather |
| **Θα βρέξει** | *(tha vré-xi)* | it will rain |
| **Θα πάω** | *(tha pá-o)* | I will go |

**Grammar Tags (Goals):**
1. Form the future with **θα** + verb.
2. Distinguish the **continuous future** (θα + present stem) from the **simple future** (θα + simple/aorist stem).

> **Pro Tip:** Use the *simple* future (θα διαβάσω) for a one-off action, and the *continuous* future (θα διαβάζω) for a repeated or ongoing one.

---

## 2. The Mutation Drill (Speed-Shift)

Read the "Base" sentence aloud. Then, look at the "Trigger" and instantly change the sentence mentally before checking the Target. This builds agility without overthinking.

| Base Sentence | Trigger | Target Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Πάω στη δουλειά.** <br> *(I go to work.)* | Make it **future** | **Θα πάω στη δουλειά.** |
| **Διαβάζω το βιβλίο.** <br> *(I read the book.)* | Future, **simple** (once) | **Θα διαβάσω το βιβλίο.** |
| **Τρώω στις οκτώ.** <br> *(I eat at eight.)* | Future, **continuous** (habit) | **Θα τρώω στις οκτώ.** |
| **Βρέχει στη Θεσσαλονίκη.** <br> *(It rains in Thessaloniki.)* | Make it **future** | **Θα βρέξει στη Θεσσαλονίκη.** |

---

## 3. The Grammar-Saturated Story

Read this short story three times: first for accuracy, second for comprehension, and third *as fast as possible*. This forces your brain to "soak" in the patterns of the future tense.

> «Αύριο θα βρέξει στη Θεσσαλονίκη. Δεν θα πάω με τα πόδια· θα πάρω το λεωφορείο. Το πρωί θα πιω έναν καφέ και θα διαβάσω τις ειδήσεις. Μετά θα δουλέψω μέχρι το μεσημέρι. Το βράδυ θα μείνω σπίτι και θα βλέπω μια ταινία.»
