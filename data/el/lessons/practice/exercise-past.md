# Unit 13-14: Pronouns & The Past

This exercise is divided into four fluency-building activities to move you from theory to automaticity.

## 1. The Constrained Picture Exercise

*(A small café in Monastiraki, Athens, with people chatting over coffee)*
![Café in Monastiraki](/assets/images/monastiraki_cafe.png)

**Power Lexis:**

| Greek | Pronunciation | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Χθες** | *(chthes)* | yesterday |
| **Πήγα** | *(pí-ga)* | I went |
| **Είδα** | *(í-da)* | I saw |
| **Τον / την / το** | *(ton / tin / to)* | him / her / it |
| **Της είπα** | *(tis í-pa)* | I told her |

**Grammar Tags (Goals):**
1. Use the **Aorist** (simple past) for completed actions and the **Imperfect** for ongoing ones.
2. Place **weak object pronouns** *before* the verb (Τον είδα, not Είδα τον).

> **Pro Tip:** Aorist = it happened and finished (είδα — I saw). Imperfect = it was happening / used to happen (έβλεπα — I was watching).

---

## 2. The Mutation Drill (Speed-Shift)

Read the "Base" sentence aloud. Then, look at the "Trigger" and instantly change the sentence mentally before checking the Target. This builds agility without overthinking.

| Base Sentence | Trigger | Target Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Βλέπω τον άντρα.** <br> *(I see the man.)* | Replace object with **pronoun** | **Τον βλέπω.** |
| **Τον βλέπω.** <br> *(I see him.)* | Make it **past** (Aorist) | **Τον είδα.** |
| **Πάω στο καφέ.** <br> *(I go to the café.)* | Make it **past** | **Πήγα στο καφέ.** |
| **Πίνω καφέ κάθε μέρα.** <br> *(I drink coffee every day.)* | Past **habit** (Imperfect) | **Έπινα καφέ κάθε μέρα.** |

---

## 3. The Grammar-Saturated Story

Read this short story three times: first for accuracy, second for comprehension, and third *as fast as possible*. This forces your brain to "soak" in the patterns of the past tenses and object pronouns.

> «Χθες πήγα σε ένα καφέ στο Μοναστηράκι. Έπινα τον καφέ μου και διάβαζα ένα βιβλίο. Είδα μια παλιά φίλη. Της είπα "γεια" και την κάλεσα στο τραπέζι μου. Μιλούσαμε για ώρες. Όταν έφυγε, την αγκάλιασα.»
